AQUARION!!!

Well, I didn't expect my first blog post to not be .hack// but oh well here we are.

To be honest I really wanted to have my blog up before I got too far into my rewatch. Even moreso now that I realize I don't remember anything from Evol. At this point I'm not even sure I watched it anymore.

I didn't mean to start a rewatch, but I started watching Myth of Emotions without actually realizing what it was until there was the signature suggestive merge. I'll try the new one again when I finish going through the old ones again but as it stands it just made me want to rewatch the original. lol. It's definitely still my favorite I think. It's just got that great older aesthetic and while it has what I think I would consider something like "blueprints for tropes" it still feels like it stands on it's own. Meanwhile Evol leaps into a more modern trope assignment. Maybe it's just being separated by eras of anime? It feels like the tropes progress similarly to .hack// in that way. Particularly if you set IMOQ next to Genesis and Evol next to G.U. Maybe it's a fate of longrunning franchises.
Anyway I love the overall feelings of the series and it's total unseriousness. It's messages on the surface feel so simple and childish, but in the times we're living in maybe more needed than ever. Giant Robot Anime is always trying to save the world but no one will listen.

Something I love about this series is that despite what you would think given the nature of the whole thing there isn't any "gag homo". At least, not in the first one. I'll get back to you on later installments but the newst one did have some very positive and supportive boyxboy crushing that made me happy. In Genesis and Evol while it ends tragically they reference the original engagement between Apollonius and Toma without even a drip of satire which is so refreshing. Downside would be that he's progressively more effeminate posessive pursuer but it never gets turned into a grossout gag. Thanks.

So... Reasons I'm not sure I finished Evol before, I do not remember the reveal that Apollo was Apollon and not Apollonius?? I get the feeling this was one of those reveals that would have pissed some people off. lol. In retrospect it all adds up perfectly, especially when more of the media introduces the concepts of mistaken past life memories because of witnessing those past lives from another perspective. There was also the point mentioned that Aquarion itself might be Apollonius.

And of course I love the fucking Ginger Dog Boy. Apollo is very cute. I also like Kagura in Evol, but I do not really care for Amata like... At all. lol. He has brunette energy.

Final question for now, what the who the fuck is Fudo.
