The Weight of The World

Before I get into weight, the first very first thing he feels, he's given the Ooze.

It feels much more in your face as a sensation compared to the gravity. Maybe because in your own every day you might not notice gravity without extra weight, but you would definitely notice if your hand was covered in slime.
Maybe it's not that deep, but maybe it is given the nature of Morganna, it makes me think of like birth goop. Considering this is also like his digital birth down there in the flesh dungeon.

Previously I pointed out a scene where Tsukasa asks Mimiru about being able to log out and how he stumbles as the soundtrack strikes an ominous cord and begins to play. Mostly appreciating the atmosphere of it.

I think this also plays into something else I observed this time. The physical weight to Tsukasa's existence within The World and how they subtly draw attention to it. All of these sensations slowly pile up throughout the series, quietly establishing his reality. Kind of like how you might take everyday sensations for granted. I just noticed gravity in particular this time.

When Tsukasa gates for example, you hear the sound of his dramatic movement, and you see the force of his weight start to pull him back down as he arrives, landing with force on his feet. Moments later however you see Mimiru gate in, but she is simply standing there. Quite dramatically different.
Comparatively, I also think of the difference when Kite gate hacks. It's also different from a standard gating, but still different from Tsukasa, he totally freezes on reentry while Tsukasa still has organic movement.

There's also a physicality to when he tries to log out later. The combinations of their choices in visuals and sounds combined with his expressions pushes this feeling of sensation. The process of logging out seems to feel good, until he is jolted out of it by hitting a barrier that also gives the impression of being a physical sensation to him. He reacts as though it might be painful with an impact sound and color before we see the visual representation of this route being closed off to him.

Right after this, we have have a direct contextualization for his bad attitude and antisocial behaviors. Fear and anxiety. His new baseline is already one of confusion and unease, but he gets jolted by something new and traumatizing. Behind him is the pressure of Mimiru's perceived pursuit and he lashes out like a cat with redirected aggression. Many such cases.

A little further along this happens yet again. Tsukasa reaches out in confusion, and in his terror of confronting the reality of his situation when asked about his terminal, lashes out at Mimiru again. He enters fight or flight, and when she keeps him from running he becomes aggressive.

Finally the conclusion of all this sensation building is that Tsukasa can feel pain.

The visual effect of having Dun Loireag's fog roll in over him as he falls deeper into fear and confusion upon this realization really helps push this scene emotionally. He is lost, and isolated. After, descending in the darkness of his birthplace and receiving the guardian from Morganna who intends to take advantage of all of his negative emotions and turmoil. After reaching the sensation of pain he experiences a fight or flight with Silver Knight in which he is injured and the guardian is activated. So now instead of lashing out verbally, there is a weapon.

I'm making a note here about this tablet and my theories on Morganna and Emma, a note about the epitaph of twilight, digitalization, the book, the key. Something to come back to later as I revisit other things. I need to formulate.

I had brought it up in my previous entry about episode 1, but I think Tsukasa's guardian is like the first prototype of the bracelet. It seems to me that throughout the series the data drain is being evolved and perfected by it's different users, similar to Aura. So while Aura is growing through the user, maybe the DD itself is too. (Thinking of Sora in BTW as a very direct example of both happening at once?)
Tsukasa's guardian is the first, primitive version. It isn't as powerful as the later forms, and requires physical contact to data drain, and has a visible physical bracelet. Although it is not also not directly connected to or fully controllable by the user. I wonder if data drain/the bracelet evolves and changes as the ability is passed between users. The next most prominent user being Kite, having a direct connection to the bracelet, and not requiring physical contact to DD. It's broken in his posession and the version Shugo receives is a physical item again, although he is wearing it himself. I wonder if this is a form in which it can be repaired in order to evolve again? Something like that.
