Series and Seasons of Love

So I finished the last two episodes of Aquarion Evol last night so my ending question of what the who the fuck is Fudo was immediately answered after I asked it. lol.

On one hand, like with Apollo it's not like it didn't work and it explains why he's such an enigma, but simultaneously I'm not really satisfied. The immediate thought that I'm left with is like, he loved Celiane right? Like he loved her enough to forsake everything and rip his damn wings off? The original promise was that they would meet again? But Fudo was never shown as being interested in reuniting with her reincarnation. Maybe part of that is that Celiane kept it in the family with her reincarnations? At least until Evol. lol oops.
The way he acts does feel appropriate for a celestial being come to earth tho I think. Strangely childlike but still really intelligent. Or something like that. In a way it feels as though they have ascended him into some kind of Godhood. I guess by Evol his lack of interest in Celiane makes more sense because by that point her reincarnation has already fallen for Apollon instead? Maybe. It doesn't really feel adressed to me.
Maybe part of his change of heart is the realization of his treatment of Toma. It does seem to be a part of the theme is Evol of realizing the consequences your actions have on other people, culminating in Mikono aplogizing to Mikage for Celiane's NTR of Toma, and then Fudo taking Mikage into himself.

I think I'm pretty satisfied with this wrap up honestly and I feel like it points to what I was saying before about the series not shying away from all kinds of love by allowing Toma and Apollonius to have this resolution. I'm really glad they didn't just dispose of Toma as an evil villain and he got to be fulfilled with his sorrows addressed. Something that really stuck with me and I'm glad they recalled it to push the point was the look on Toma's face as he's begging for Apollonius to come back. Very pro harm reduction feeling messages from the love robot anime.

Further, to the point of all love, while Evol was very heteronormative feeling compared to Genesis, I still felt like it carried this torch for love in an all encompassing way. In another instance there's Andy and Mix, and while he does have a small crisis about it (I don't think this is a bad thing, it's never portrayed as anything negative about Mix herself, and Andy is seemingly really upset with himself more than anything), he comes to full confident conclusion that he would still love her even if she had been turned into a boy. I feel like this could be looked at through quite a few different queer lenses in a positive light. Unfortunately I guess it's what the fuck ever because at the very end she's just back to appearing as before with no explaination, but I'm just gonna take it as a happy MTF allegory. Maybe she just got her boobs done. Good for her!
