So, I have started rereading DNAngel. If you haven't seen me yapping about it on socials, it was THE first manga I EVER read. My first exposure to manga was one of the infamous "How to draw Manga" books the seemed to sneak their way into book fairs all over. Albeit the seemingly less popular red edition, not blue.
On a subsequent trip to a Books A Million, I saw the words MANGA in bold letters signifying a whole section. More drawing books! was my thought as I made an excited beeline to the aisle, only to be met with a whole new world.
I'm not sure if DNAngel was the first thing I picked up, but with it's flashy holographic spine it might have been. The cover would only pull me in even more, it was so quintessentially anime. It was everything the how to draw book I had been copying wished it was. Even now the early volumes still feel like an ideal to me, even if volume one feels like it's finding it's footing a little.
I spent years mimicking Yukiru Sugisaki, poorly. I was obsessed with capturing the way she drew faces in particular, focusing on that to my detriment, drawing inflated heads with beautiful eyes on formless bodies.
Looking at the series again now I can see that beyond stylistic influence, I seem to have picked up a lot more in taste and sensibilities than I realized. Seeing the way she draws Daisuke, the fashion, the moe instincts and focus type... I didn't stand a chance. Even now I look at Daisuke and the favorites I've had since him and the kind of things I still draw and it's like a bright red string.

I've been rereading the series thru my Japanese volumes, which I had picked up while I was there in 2018. The moment I saw them I had no hesitation, suitcase space be damned. I've read up to the point I owned in english already, I only collected up to vol 6 originally before life got in the way of my otaku activities. I found a pretty good deal on 7-11 so I'll be rereading those in English when they arrive~ I hope I can find 12 and 13 for a decent price but they seem to be pretty rare since it was closer to the hiatus and potentially the collapse of Tokyopop.

With the announcement of the new editions coming out in english, I felt compelled to start rereading it. I really prefer reading physical manga, and finally having the ending as a physcial book feels like the right time to finish the series despite it being wrapped up over 4 years ago now.
I'm feeling pretty torn about waiting for the new english releases to finish reading it. I think I will though, because I feel like reading it in my hands will give me to most complete and fulfilling experience. I don't think reading a scanlation will do justice to my beginnings.
Also, it's a chance for what feels like a rare opportunity to wait for releases again and really sit with a series instead of consuming it all at once, which I think kind of cheapens it. This is topping off over two decades, and something that was truly an event horizon in my life, so I will treat it with care.
