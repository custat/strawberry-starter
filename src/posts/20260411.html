"Starting over from Z.E.R.O"

A lot of people have immediately latched onto this, which doesn't surprise me at all. They've been chomping at the bit for any excuse to dismiss S3 for over a decade. lol
Now, when Piroshi said he wants the name to reflect "restarting from zero" that did concern me a bit, but he also insists that long time fans will be satisfied.

In a more recent interview, the question of if players should revisit older installments is raised to Miyazaki. His reply to which kind of confirms to me that season 4 will be following the pattern of previous installments. In that you can fully enjoy this game standalone, but the underbelly will be connected to the continually intertwining subplot.
I think they just really want to push the idea of it being a fresh start disconnected in order to get new people to play it without worrying about the massive iceberg that is the rest of the franchise. I'm sure it's really daunting if you're not an obsessive pilled person like me lol.
Considering they tried the opposite approach with Link as the last standalone game where they tried to find a creative way to repackage the existing story for new players, it makes sense to me that they would try the complete opposite this time.
Also, considering that they've still been building on the existing .hack// story through webnovels up until 2020 I feel like it would be weird to completely toss it all. If nothing else they may scoot around the banco IPs by subtly connecting it to the past series like with FUGA and Solatorobo (and maybe .hack// itself lmao). Leaving the consipiracy work to trufflepigs like me.

All this to say I will be approaching my theories with the lense that this is taking place in like 2035 after Versus or something.

Morgaura

"What happened to Aura?"

Well, not whatever is happening in the trailer I don't think haha

Considering these are her last canon appearances... I don't think the new lolibait Aura is Aura at all.

While this new look for "Aura" has this smaller, younger looking frame... The entire plot of .hack//sekai no mukou ni centers around using Sora's bio element (brain) to repair and ultimately, we can assume, complete Aura. This ends up also giving her the appearance of an adult woman. After which she is whisked away by Kusame and stuck in a birdcage in David's literal thumbdrive. Which he dies protecting ensuring that she is picked up by Tanaka, who is directed to the middle of Yosemite National Park by Tokio in a dream. Unless MAMA somehow still got their hands on her after all of that, I think we're looking at someone else here. This series is certainly no stranger to Aura offshoots of varying types, as well as other parallel advanced AI sprouting from the premordial soup of the data sea. I have my thoughts about who she is and I'll dig into that more next.

Morganna

Throughout the series we've had multiple attempts to control and recreate Aura, and I think that comes back into play yet again here. After we get a glimpse of "Aura" we get a dramatic shot of Skeith's wand, chained like the statue of Aura has always been. (plus some cord?) With a feather turning from white to black. I haven't decided what I think about the feather, it could simply be transformation to evil Aura and I'm fucking stupid. It also makes me think of the fact that Haseo is portrayed as having wings in the novels. But. Bringing this back to my thinking that this girl is in fact Morganna.

We know there are currently two factions within MAMA. One, pro Aura presumably led by Genius, since Dominique de Mirabeau is dead, wants to reinstate the true Aura to have her accomplish their goals. The other, anti Aura led by Veronica, wants to create a new form of Aura they can control. Now with Veronica being the one leading the anti Aura faction and the potential of this girl being a reborn Morganna, there is also a history of Veronica tampering with Morganna. In the New Testament web novel she tampers with the programming.

The last attempt to create a new Aura was the RA plan during R:2 which produced the epitaphs. It failed, and resulted in the spitaph users instead. The epitaphs themselves are pieces of Morganna, and in Last Recode they are all united within Haseo. If the imitation Tarvos isn't enough, Ryou now works under Reiko who has the true Tarvos, meaning all the true pieces are gathered together. Whether the proximity or the fact that the phases are combined through Haseo is enough, I'm wondering if this was the missing component they needed for something like the RA plan to succeed, resulting in recreating Morganna through Haseo's PC combined with AIDA. Further to this point, Morgaura's black dipped arms and legs, as well as her black dress. While evil Aura could still be a potential, the way she acts through her body langauge feels very Morganna to me, simply. lol

We know for sure that Morganna is involved at the very least, as was revealed in some dialogue between two new offline characters. (Who feel like they're going to fill a Ryuuji and David sort of role)

The dialogue as translated by falions:
1. I wasn’t aware your taste in cars was so... vintage.
2. The older it is, the harder it is for Morganna to interfere.
3. Yes, there’s not even a GPS in here...

They also mention not being able to be reached by Morganna due to the car being analogue... Which brings me to my next bit of theory.

Immortal Dusk

The codename for .hack//Z.E.R.O. and it's related works is "Project Dusk". In .hack//Link the mass Real Digitalization event that occurs is called "Immortal Dusk". We have our analogue warriors up there getting around in a techless car in order to avoid Morganna amid a world that appears to be teeming with an over abundance of advancement probably expanded from similar ideas as the special zone in quantum. In interviews CC2 is talking about a much greater focus on the real world story, beyond just news and chatrooms. It feels like it's implying some dual experiences. Crossing of borders perhaps. There are a few possibilities for how they go about this.

My attention is drawn to Kite_0's headphones immediately upon his first appearance. After drawing them a few times they've grown on me, but they were really jarring at first! I think this design choice may actually serve a purpose though. His entire design gives a very modern "irl" impression. At first glance it immediately makes me think of Tokio. Adidas joggers, cyber tactical jacket, sneakers that look like a modernized version of Tokio's, etc. I even joked that his outfit looks like something I would wear to step out of the house. My point being that he looks like he doesn't actually belong in The World. We'll have to see more of the in game character designs to know for sure, maybe the customizations have become more lax in this revision. So far though all of the other designs we've seen seem to follow the usual general fantasy kind of feeling. Both "Balmung" and "Blackrose", and the Tu Tribe looking designs feel like they fall into an aesthetic set.
There are a couple of things on his design that point to in game aesthetics though. His weapons, hair, and wave. I think these can still be explained away though. On top of maintaining strict in-game aesthetics (ie not too extreme of item shop looking characters) CC2 has also always done a really good job of keeping their irl characters feeling really believable, with a few anime interpretations of realistic features. (ie Misono's blue hair that I interpret as being stylized black, or the extremity of Tokio's red hair and golden eyes that I think is just stylization of him actually being a ginger potentially related to his being a doubleware)
I think looking at the overall aesthetic to his design there is a chance that he could be an actual hacker that tampered with the look of his character himself, which would be a first for a MC. Just as an aside.

His hair is obviously not a natural color, but their choice to make it streaked feels like an indication that it's dyed, moreso than being and in game hair color. (thought it still could be). It also immediately reminded me of Yoko in The Animatrix Beyond. Along with the hair they both have the orangish left cheek markings. have Although the coloring is inverted. I have to wonder if this is intentional considering that they're talking about wanting to incorporate the real world into the game more, and Beyond takes place in an area within the borderline of The Matrix itself. Kind of like a nod indicating Kite_0 existing between two worlds himself.

His wave is the most damning piece of his design as far as PC vs real boy goes. If he is a PC that's really all there is to it. If he is from outside though, two possibilities come to mind. One, maybe he's super chuunibyou like Tokio and put it on his cheek himself. The other, I think of Sora and how she has waves appear only after receiving power, although not as a permanent feature. Maybe he receives a wave after being digitized for some reason.

His weapons are pretty easily dismissable of their own, Tokio also used in game weapons despite being a real boy. The thing that stands out to me though is that he seems to be able to take them in and out of inventory. Due to game limitations though, I don't think we know if Tokio himself can do something like that too.

In Link, (iirc) Immortal Dusk is triggered by Aura's song, and here we have a new "Kite" wearing these headphones. In the promo art Morgaura's hands are reaching down around them while he wears a peaceful expression, embraced by her song perhaps?
What I'm saying is, maybe Kite_0 isn't a doubleware, but digitized throught Morgaura's song.

Data Drain

The trailer ends with Kite_0 performing a data drain that looks like an amalgamation of previous DD, plus this energy core with a similar negative space coloring scheme to AIDA. (bringing it back to the AIDA from 5th form Haseo within Morgaura perhaps?)
The thing that stood out to me thought is that he is data draining, presumably, other players. He is data draining people. Something that even New Testament Kite makes a conscious choice not to do because of the implications. This is interesting to me. Clearly we have no idea why he's doing this but it's an established taboo from the very beginning when Tsukasa's guardian DD'd Silver Knight.
